Next steps:

     through Flux ImagePolicy):

         flux install --components=source-controller,kustomize-controller,image-reflector-controller

  2. Define an ImageRepository + ImagePolicy for each app you want Kuberik
     to track.

  3. Create your first Rollout. See:

         https://github.com/kuberik/kuberik/blob/main/examples/basic-rollout/

  4. Inspect:

         kuberik get rollouts -A
         kuberik get gates -A
         kuberik get healthchecks -A
